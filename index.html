A dedicated, Ministry-branded customer support platform for managing stakeholder enquiries, complaints and service requests.

November 19, 2026 | Prepared for Ministry of Trade

Thank you for taking the time to explore Mitra Helpdesk. We are pleased to demonstrate how the platform can help the Ministry of Trade manage enquiries, complaints and support requests from businesses, traders, importers, exporters, investors and other stakeholders through one organised support environment. With a dedicated Ministry-branded workspace, the Ministry's support team can coordinate requests, monitor progress, improve response times and provide a more consistent stakeholder experience.

Your Helpdesk. Your Brand. Our Platform.

The Ministry receives its own dedicated, branded environment within Mitra Helpdesk. Its stakeholders can access the Ministry's support channels, while authorised Ministry support officers manage incoming requests, communicate with customers and track cases through to resolution. Each organisation using Mitra Helpdesk operates within its own tenant, keeping its users, customers, tickets and organisational data separate from other organisations on the platform.

Overcoming the Support Chaos

  • Centralise Stakeholder Enquiries: Bring enquiries, complaints and support requests from businesses and other stakeholders into one organised helpdesk, reducing fragmented communication and helping prevent missed or unattended requests.
  • Improve Request Handling: Assign requests to the appropriate Ministry support officers, track progress and maintain clear communication from submission to resolution.
  • Strengthen Accountability: Use reporting, audit trails and AI-assisted support to improve visibility, monitor outstanding requests and maintain control over support operations.
